About Me

22 year old Front End Developer. I began my web developer journey in Q3 of 2022 as a side project alongside my engineering university degree at the time. For quite some time, I have never really considered coding for websites, or in any language, to be a career path I wanted. The majority of my academic years leading up to now primarily revolved around my passion for creating and building new ideas I had in my head. Practical things, technical things, and just random devices just because I thought they were cool. It is what lead me to engineering in university after all. It was a mindset I had where my art was imbued through physical creations, and programming was purely a means for functionality.

Looking back, I believe my mindset was of a stubborn kind more than anything else. An unwillingness to change, latching onto the idea that engineering was the be all and end all. Ultimately, the difficult work and lack of reward I felt ended with me beginning to resent my choices in university. I wanted something different. I did various side projects to take my mind of it, but one remained relevant to me.

Coding was something I could do quite well whenever it came up as a part of my education; and, when it did, I exceeded. I adapted fast and absorbed programming concepts and methods without fail, but paid no mind to it outside of my enclosed engineering workspace. This part of me has changed. Where I felt disheartened in my current work, I felt rewarded and accomplished in coding my web site projects. I realised my passion for creating whatever was in my mind wasn't limited to the physical realm. It was from here I changed my direction in life. The work, though difficult at times, was never bothersome or exhausting; it felt like something I could do forever.

And now back to the present I sit here typing away, thinking of solutions to all my projects and probably after I finish this paragraph. (I hope you enjoyed my ted talk)
